# Data Agent — 5  questions (ask cold, no explanation)

1. Show me all regions with predicted energy deficiencies during tomorrow's peak hours.
2. What is the current demand as a percentage of available capacity, and is that healthy?
3. Which feeders are above 80% loading right now, and which region are they in?
4. Are any critical or medical-baseline customers currently on an outaged feeder?
5. What's our available peaker headroom compared to the current reserve margin, and would starting another peaker make sense right now?
